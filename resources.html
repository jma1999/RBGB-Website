Featured Course

Energy Modeling in eQUEST

This course introduces new hires to the role of energy modeling in high-performance building projects, with a practical focus on eQUEST workflows, assumptions, inputs, outputs, and quality review.

Learning Goal

Understand why energy models are built

Learn how models support code compliance, LEED points, design decisions, energy strategy, cost evaluation, and performance optimization.

Software Focus

Build confidence in eQUEST

Get familiar with project setup, geometry, envelope, schedules, HVAC systems, utility rates, simulations, and reports.

Project Skill

Translate drawings into model inputs

Practice reading architectural, mechanical, lighting, and envelope information and converting it into clear modeling assumptions.
