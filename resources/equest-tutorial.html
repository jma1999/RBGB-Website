Energy Modeling in eQUEST

Beginner Course | eQUEST Workflow | New Hire Resource

This course introduces new hires to the role of energy modeling in high-performance building projects, with a practical focus on eQUEST workflows, project setup, assumptions, simulation outputs and quality review.

The goal is not only to learn the software, but to understand how an energy model supports code compliance, LEED documentation, design decisions, energy strategy and performance optimization.
