Coffee Regional Medical Center

Douglas, GA | Energy Audit

RBGB conducted an in-depth energy and water audit of this 170,000 ft2 regional hospital, which was recently won the No. 1 Small Hospital in Georgia award.

The investigation identified several minor and major efficiency improvements, including recommendations for improved sustainable facility management practices.

The hospital was planning a major expansion and, as predicted through energy calculations, the hospital was able to add 25,000 ft2 of new patient care space and still have a lower annual energy bill than before the expansion.

This was achieved because the hospital implemented many of the energy efficiency recommendations, re-organized the operations and maintenance department and built the new addition using high performance building criteria.
