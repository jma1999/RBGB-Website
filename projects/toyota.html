LEED Registered

Southeast Toyota Vehicle Processing Facility

Commerce, GA | Energy Modeling / Commissioning / LEED Administration

Completed in 2020, this extensive project is registered as a LEED version 4 “Group” project and consists of five main buildings, support structures, and an extensive site.

The project Owner is dedicated to sustainable design and construction and will be installing a 1MW solar power system on the site.

Infrastructure is also in place for a future rainharvest system to provide for toilet flushing and car washing.

RBGB provided all LEED Administration services, Energy Simulation, and Commissioning services.
