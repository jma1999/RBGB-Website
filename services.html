Services

Performance-focused services for better buildings.

LEED Administration Plus

End-to-end LEED coordination, credit strategy, documentation management, and team support to help projects move smoothly from early goals to certification.

Commissioning

Verification of building systems through planning, testing, documentation, and issue tracking to ensure systems operate as intended.

Energy Modeling

Building performance simulation for design optimization, code compliance, LEED support, lifecycle analysis, and energy reduction strategies.

Facility-level energy assessments that identify operational inefficiencies, savings opportunities, and practical retrofit recommendations.

Sustainable Facility Management

Support for existing buildings through performance tracking, operational sustainability, maintenance strategy, and occupant comfort improvements.

Training & Workshops

Custom workshops for project teams, facility managers, students, and organizations on green building systems, LEED, commissioning, energy modeling, and sustainable operations.
